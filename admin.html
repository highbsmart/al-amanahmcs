Expunge Member

This permanently deletes their login and every record tied to them — savings, admin charges, loans, transactions, payslip history — with nothing left to restore. Their Al-Amanah No. becomes free for reuse. This cannot be undone.

Set Sign-in Email

This is the address they'll type into their officer/admin portal login screen, alongside their password. Their Al-Amanah No., savings, and loan history are untouched — this only changes what they sign in with.

Edit Member Details

Corrects details entered incorrectly or left blank when this member was registered. Al-Amanah No. is edited separately from the Management Team tab (officers) or is fixed at registration for ordinary members.

Needed for "Forgot password" to work on the Member Login page. Leave blank to leave their current email (if any) unchanged.

Fix Al-Amanah No.

Use this to correct an officer profile that was set up with a placeholder number (e.g. TREAS-001, ADMIN-001) instead of their real Al-Amanah No. This changes only the number — their savings, loans, and login are untouched. Once corrected, this is the number they'll use to log back in as an ordinary member if their role is ever reassigned to someone else.
