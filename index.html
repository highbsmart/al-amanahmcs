Who we are

A cooperative built on Amanah — trust.

Al-Amanah Multi-Purpose Co-operative Society is registered by and operates in affiliation with the Kwara State College of Arabic and Islamic Legal Studies (KwaraCAILS), Ilorin. Membership is open to staff of the College, who contribute monthly savings and, in turn, gain access to interest-conscious, transparent loan facilities administered by an elected management committee.

Every application, approval, decline and deduction is recorded against your Al-Amanah number, so you always know exactly what you have saved, what you owe, and what remains available to you.

Loan products

Three facilities, one Amanah.

Each loan type carries its own eligible multiplier, administrative charge and repayment window — all calculated against your savings balance.

🏠

Real Loan

Up to 3× your savings for personal or business needs, over a fixed 24 months — no extra fee beyond the standard 7.5% monthly savings administrative charge.

🛒

Commodity Loan

Flat maximum of ₦500,000 for household items, vehicles or equipment, plus a 10% commodity fee, over a fixed 12 months.

🤝

Humanitarian Loan

Flat maximum of ₦100,000 for medical, education or bereavement needs, over a fixed 8 months with priority review.

How it works

From application to disbursement.

01

Apply

Log in with your Al-Amanah number and submit a loan request online.

02

Review

The management committee reviews your savings history and eligibility.

03

Decision

Your application is approved or declined, with a reason if declined.

04

Disbursed

Approved funds are released and a repayment schedule is activated.

05

Repay

Monthly deductions reduce your balance until the loan is cleared.
