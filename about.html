Our story

Built on trust, run by members.

Al-Amanah Multi-Purpose Co-operative Society was formed by staff of the Kwara State College of Arabic and Islamic Legal Studies (KwaraCAILS), Ilorin, to promote thrift and provide accessible, transparent credit to colleagues within the institution.

Our mission

To mobilise the savings of KwaraCAILS staff and deploy them responsibly through Real, Commodity and Humanitarian loan facilities — administered with the same trust ("Amanah") that gives the society its name.

Our affiliation

The Society operates in affiliation with the Kwara State College of Arabic and Islamic Legal Studies, Ilorin, and membership is drawn from the College's academic and non-academic staff. Governance rests with an elected management committee that reviews every loan application against each member's savings history.

⚖️

Governance

An elected management committee reviews applications, approves or declines loans, and reports to members at the Annual General Meeting.

💰

Membership

Open to confirmed staff of KwaraCAILS. Members contribute monthly savings and become eligible for loans after a qualifying period.
